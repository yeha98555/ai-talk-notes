A Hugging Face Journal Club session discussing a paper on asynchronous on-policy distillation (AsyncOPD). On-policy distillation normally has the student generate the samples the teacher then scores, keeping the training signal on the student's current policy; making that loop asynchronous decouples sample generation from the optimizer to raise throughput, at the cost of training on samples drawn from a slightly older ("stale") policy. The paper's central question is how much staleness on-policy distillation can tolerate before the drift between the sampling policy and the policy being trained outweighs the speedup that asynchrony buys.
